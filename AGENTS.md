# Brand strategy is a product requirement

Before proposing, designing, or implementing any new feature, read `BRAND_BIBLE.md` and treat its principles as acceptance criteria—not optional visual guidance.

Every new feature must:

- strengthen Curated's position as a private style house for the individual, rather than a productivity app, inventory tool, marketplace, or generic AI product;
- demonstrate discernment, hospitality, discretion, personal continuity, and stewardship over consumption;
- remain wardrobe-first, private by default, explainable, body-respectful, and honest about uncertainty;
- use the brand voice and luxury service standards defined in `BRAND_BIBLE.md` across happy paths, empty states, permissions, errors, loading, correction, export, disconnection, and deletion;
- preserve customer agency and avoid false urgency, coercive engagement, hidden commercial influence, or unnecessary collection of personal data;
- express “maximalist with restraint”: richness in story, material, and emotion, with discipline in hierarchy, interaction, and editing;
- work as a clear, accessible product experience even when decorative brand elements are removed.

Feature proposals and implementation reviews must include a brief brand-alignment check. If a feature conflicts with `BRAND_BIBLE.md`, revise or reject the feature rather than weakening the brand principles. Do not modify `BRAND_BIBLE.md` merely to justify a conflicting feature; strategic changes require explicit founder direction.
