# Legacy Recommendation Containment Plan

Status: proposed, non-deployed, 2026-08-02.

## Objective

Keep Current Founder Preview available for workflow and continuity testing while making no claim that it is a V2 recommendation-quality environment. Keep Production unchanged and keep V2 Evaluation disabled until its release gates pass.

## Containment boundaries

1. Freeze legacy recommendation authority. Do not graft V2 suppression, memory, Dressing Posture, composition, or adjudication behavior onto it.
2. Do not add Founder-, garment-, venue-, or scenario-specific rules and do not tune legacy scores.
3. Treat suppression bypasses and overstated persistence language as trust incidents. Preserve sanitized evidence in the governed evaluation suite.
4. Use the legacy characterization suite to document known blocked behavior. Never describe a passing characterization detector as a passing recommendation-quality requirement.
5. Do not change customer-facing copy without Product review. Until then, operational reports must state the actual authority: consultation-only, event/today, similar contexts, or until restored.
6. Keep Current Preview online for workflows, persistence, navigation, and continuity checks. Pause Founder outfit-quality approval there.
7. Activate Founder-only V2 Evaluation only after the isolated security, owner-scope, allowlist, canonical read-only access, artifact/cache isolation, kill-switch, rollback, and Current Preview continuity gates pass.

## Safe operational response

- Do not promise that the legacy engine will learn V2 behavior.
- Do not silently clear or rewrite a failed correction or suppression.
- Preserve the customer submission when an operational request fails and distinguish persistence success from regeneration success.
- Record only privacy-safe trace identifiers and bounded failure stages; do not copy private schedule, venue, address, Profile, or wardrobe content into fixtures or logs.

This plan proposes no deployment, activation, migration, or Production change.
